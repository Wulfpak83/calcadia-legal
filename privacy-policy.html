Calcadia Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

Calcadia is a nutrition tracking app. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a hashed password, used to create and sign in to your account. Authentication is handled by Google Firebase.
  • Profile and goals. Details you enter during setup, such as calorie and macro targets and meal preferences.
  • Food, water and weight logs. The meals, foods, water and weigh-ins you record in the app.
  • Meal photos. When you photograph a meal, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini API to identify the food and estimate nutrition. We do not save the photo on our servers.
  • Voice logging. When you log food by voice, your iPhone turns your speech into text on the device. The recording is not uploaded. The text is sent to Google's Gemini API to work out the foods and nutrition.
  • Purchase information. Subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. We receive subscription status, not your payment details.

How we use it

Your data is used to run the app: calculating your daily totals, generating suggestions, syncing your logs across sessions, and maintaining your subscription. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where your data lives

Your logs and profile are stored in a Supabase database. Authentication data is held by Firebase. Photo and voice content is processed through Google's Gemini API. Subscription status is held by RevenueCat and Apple.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings > Delete Account inside the app. This removes your profile, logs, and account credentials from our systems. Subscription billing is managed by Apple; cancel there to stop renewals.

If you delete your account, we keep a one-way code made from your sign-in ID. It can't be turned back into your email or name. We use it only to stop repeat free trials, and we delete it after 24 months.

Health disclaimer

Calcadia provides nutrition estimates and suggestions for informational purposes. It is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making significant dietary changes.

Children

Calcadia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
